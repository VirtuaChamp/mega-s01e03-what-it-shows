The tickets

  1. parser: parser reads day-first dates month first. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. Day and month swap in the app, in email and in booking-v2 forms. It covers the parser row above, including the reports in the note under the table. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  2. hint: booking form date hint shows month first but the form reads day first. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. This happens only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants, and a parser fix does not touch it. It covers the hint row above, which had no ticket of its own. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

How the count is made

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 treats reports as one issue when their subject lines match, and nobody reads the body. The same subjects come in for both bugs: “Date is wrong” and “Wrong day on the invite” each appear under parser and hint. The subject cannot tell the bugs apart. Tenant and channel can, as the cause check shows. Changing the rule needs a new decision record, because 0001 stays as it was accepted.
