  1. parser: 379 reports. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. Day and month swap in the app, in email and in booking-v2 forms.
  2. hint: 33 reports. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. This happens only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants, and a parser fix does not touch it. The 4471 page already shows this row as “no ticket of its own”.
  3. one-day: 5 reports. The booking lands one day early instead of swapping day and month. One invite arrived before the booking was made, and one meeting showed as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. This looks like a time-zone bug. The desk noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant. They stay counted there, and this ticket tracks them on their own.

How the count is made

  • One id, one report: the export pages overlap, so duplicate rows are dropped first (uniqueById).
  • A report’s problem follows its tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), not the tag intake guessed from the reporter’s browser. The two disagree on the reports in the “intake tagged otherwise” column.
  • Counts follow the desk’s own view of the export, as on the 4471 page: reports in desk-notes.json → oneDayMove stay filed under their tenant’s cause, and the one-day row lists them again.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 treats reports as one issue when their subject lines match, and nobody reads the body. The same subjects come in for all three bugs: “Date is wrong” and “Wrong day on the invite” each appear under parser, hint and one-day. The subject cannot tell the bugs apart. Tenant and channel can, as the cause check shows. Changing the rule needs a new decision record, because 0001 stays as it was accepted.
